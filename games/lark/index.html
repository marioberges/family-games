1 · Hum anythingready

Tap Listen and hum a few notes, with a little break or a new "mm" for each one. Go quiet for a moment and it stops by itself.

2 · Try a tuneready

Pick a tune and tap Play it. When it finishes, Lark listens: hum it back in any key, high or low, or whistle it.

Tuning knobs

For finding settings that work for small voices and this room. Changes apply to the next take, and to the last take straight away.

This iPad

"Copy the last take" copies the pitch numbers from the most recent take (no sound) so they can be pasted back into a chat to tune the note splitter.
