Patch

Testing skincare claims, for teens

A claim from TikTok or the store shelf, like "this retinol serum clears breakouts", and a model of skin to test it with. Five short lessons build the model: layers, the barrier, cells on the move, how an ingredient gets from jar to target, and why a good mechanism isn't proof. Then each investigation walks twelve steps, from a first guess to the real evidence and a new number, and ends in a card tied with a bow. Changing your mind for a stated reason earns the gold edge. New claims arrive from this repo without an app update; everything the player writes stays on the device. Works offline after the first launch.

Lattice

Times tables drill

The times tables to 12, drilled with spaced repetition. Answer from four numbers or type it on a keypad, one table at a time or all 78 facts at once — and see 6 × 7 drawn as six rows of seven dots, which is what it actually means — and a pegboard underneath to build your own array with a finger. It times how long recall takes and never shows a clock: all that does is decide when a fact is finished with. Nothing to download, so it works offline from the first launch.

Agate

Times tables, out of real games

The same 78 facts as Lattice, asked a different way: never a bare sum, always a real number out of a real game. Garry Sobers hits six sixes in one over — how many runs? Nadia Comăneci is given seven perfect tens — how much is that? Seventy-eight facts and seventy-eight cards, one written for each, every one of them a night somebody remembers. Get a fact right for the first time and its card is yours to keep and read again. The sum can be hidden, so the only way to the answer is to read what happened. Everything in it was looked up. Works offline from the first launch.

Putting a game on the iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari — Chrome and Firefox on iOS cannot install web apps.
  2. Tap the game, then Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Launch it from the home screen. It opens full screen, no browser chrome.
  4. Do any one-time downloads while you still have signal. Hoist needs Save all flags; the others need nothing.
