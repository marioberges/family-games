Hoist

Flag recognition drill

197 country flags, drilled with spaced repetition. Answer from four names, or find the country on a world map — the map zooms to whichever slice of the world you pick, from one region up to all 197. Progress is saved between sessions, and once the flag art is downloaded it all runs in airplane mode.

Oath

US presidents

All 45 presidents, in order, three ways. Timeline asks who was 16th, or what number Lincoln was, with a strip of their neighbours that fills in once you answer. Who Am I gives a clue in the president’s own voice, climbing from the famous to the odd true detail one rung at a time. Born Where puts them on a map of all fifty states, then asks for the town. Drag the portrait into place or pick from four. Every card won goes on a wall laid out in order, and seven cards seal a president. Every fact was looked up; the portraits are public domain. Works offline from the first launch.

Hopper

Guess the rule

A machine with a secret rule. Feed it a number, turn the crank, and see what comes out; every try goes on a paper tape. When you think you know, build the rule from tiles (times 2, then add 1) and the machine checks it on every number, not just the ones you tried. Get it wrong and it shows you one number where your rule and its rule disagree. Seven levels climb from adding to numbers multiplied by themselves, with negative numbers on the way. Each cracked rule is a blueprint on the workshop wall, and cracking one from just enough clues earns a gold gear. Works offline from the first launch.

Romp

Doodles that come alive

Pick a place, draw a little character with the Apple Pencil or a finger, and let it go. It drops in and starts moving about, and its shape decides how: round ones roll, tall ones jump and wobble, long ones crawl and can lie across a gap like a bridge. Three places to draw for — steps up to a high shelf, two cliffs with a gap, a hill with a low tunnel — and a star in each to reach. Draw another and they meet. Grab one to toss it, tap one to make it hop. Name the ones you love to keep them in your book, change them later, and drop them into any place. Or tap Squiggle: finish a random squiggle into a monster, a face, anything, or make one for a friend to finish, and send your favourites to the book to romp. Works offline from the first launch.

Putting a game on the iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari — Chrome and Firefox on iOS cannot install web apps.
  2. Tap the game, then Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Launch it from the home screen. It opens full screen, no browser chrome.
  4. Do any one-time downloads while you still have signal. Hoist needs Save all flags; the others need nothing.
